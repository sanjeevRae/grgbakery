# THE GrG BAKERY & CAFÉ
### Freshly Baked • Crafted with Love • Served Fresh

**Welcome to The GrG Bakery**

The GrG Bakery is a local bakery and café in Lalitpur, Nepal, offering a delightful selection of freshly prepared cakes, pastries, desserts, savoury snacks and beverages. What started as a small home-baking venture by founder Binay Gurung grew into a bakery and café where people can enjoy delicious treats, relax and spend time with friends.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, ordering a customized cake, enjoying your favourite coffee or simply craving something sweet, The GrG Bakery aims to make every visit special.

**Our specialties include:**
- Freshly prepared cakes and cheesecakes
- Customized birthday and celebration cakes
- Pastries, brownies and desserts
- Fresh bakery items and savoury snacks
- Hot coffee, iced coffee and refreshing drinks
